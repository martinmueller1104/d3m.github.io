Benjamín Arias is a Mechanical Engineering student at the Rosario National University (UNR) in Argentina. As part of the KOSPIE scholarship, he joined the Chair of Funtional Materials at Saarland University in October 2025, starting his master thesis that is jointly supervised by the data-driven materials design group.
His master thesis focuses on applying machine learning for microstructure analysis in low-data conditions.
He is currently completing a research internship at Volkswagen AG in Wolfsburg, focusing on warm forming automotive steel characterization.


**Current Focus Areas:**
  - Machine learning for microstructure segmentation
